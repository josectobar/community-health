Practice: The Retail Floor

Sam works evenings at an electronics store. A customer becomes angry over a returned item, yells, and throws a display box that hits Sam's shoulder. The store has one associate on the floor at closing and no policy for handling hostile customers.
  1. Part (a): Who is the perpetrator, and what is their relationship to the store? Which type does that make it?
  2. Part (b): Pick one prevention category and name a specific action the store could have taken beforehand.
  3. Part (c): Is this an injury or an illness? Name two ways it could affect Sam.
(a) Is the customer served by the store? What category does "someone the organization serves" point to?

(b) Could a change to the space, the policy, or a person's behavior have reduced the risk? Is your action specific (not just "be safer")?

(c) Did the harm happen in one moment or build up over time? Beyond the shoulder, could there be emotional or performance effects?
