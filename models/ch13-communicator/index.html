Program: 6-Month Community Walking Program for Adults with Prediabetes (City Parks + Health Department Partnership)
Process
Average attendance: 4.2 of 6 sessions. Three sites ran well; one site had consistent facilitator turnover. 18% of participants dropped after session 1.
Impact
Mean daily steps increased 2,100 steps/day (p<0.01). HbA1c improved modestly (-0.3%) among completers. Self-efficacy for exercise improved significantly.
Impact
Participants with <3 sessions attended showed no significant improvement. Dropout was highest among participants working evening shifts.
Outcome (5-yr follow-up needed)
Long-term diabetes incidence data not yet available. Trend data promising but inconclusive.
Process / Equity
Program underserved Spanish-speaking residents: only 2 of 3 Spanish-language sites were fully operational. Recruitment was lower in highest-need zip codes.
Recommendation
Program shows meaningful impact for completers. Key actions: address facilitator retention at Site 4, expand Spanish-language outreach, and consider scheduling options for shift workers.
