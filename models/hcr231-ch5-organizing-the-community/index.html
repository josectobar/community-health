Meet the community: Rio Lindo

Your activity is set in one place. Get familiar with it now so you can jump straight into problem-solving in class.

Rio Lindo is a rural community of about 4,200 residents in Santa Cruz County. Most residents speak Spanish at home, and many work at the local produce packing sheds. County data show that emergency-room visits for heat illness doubled last summer. Imagine you are a health education specialist newly assigned there — you don't know anyone yet, and you'll work with this community for the next two years.
