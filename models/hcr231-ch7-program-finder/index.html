Who does each program actually serve?

The distinguishing feature is almost always who is eligible, not what the program provides.
ProgramServesThe detail people miss
WICPregnant women, new and breastfeeding mothers, infants under 1, children under 5Immigration status does not affect eligibility. It provides food and nutrition services — it is not health insurance.
MedicaidLow-income individuals and familiesIt is health insurance. Ten states have not expanded it, which leaves many low-income pregnant women uncovered.
CHIPChildren whose families earn too much for Medicaid but cannot afford private coverageIt exists to fill the gap above the Medicaid line — that gap is the whole point of the program.
Title XLow-income people seeking family planning servicesFamily planning and reproductive health — contraception, screening. Not food, not insurance.

How a written answer earns full credit

The case questions on this quiz are worth 2 points each, and they are scored on application, not recall. Every one of them follows the same four-move structure:

  1. Name the term exactly as it appears in the Key Terms. "Transportation barrier" earns the point. "She can't really get there" does not.
  2. Label it by type when the question asks you to. If the prompt says one communication barrier and one access barrier, write the labels down. Graders look for them.
  3. Anchor it to a specific detail from the case. Point at the sentence in the scenario that proves it. Generic reasoning that could apply to any patient loses half the point.
  4. Answer every part of the prompt. Several questions ask for three things — a bridge, a cultural value, and something you'd want to learn about the community. Count the asks before you submit.

Try it on a practice case

Practice case — not on the quiz. Deysi is 19 and four months pregnant. She reads Spanish comfortably but little English. The nearest prenatal clinic is on a bus route, but the route stops running before her shift ends. Her mother has told her that women in their family have always given birth without doctors and that going in only invites problems. Deysi missed her first appointment and has not rescheduled.
