Run a real search

This one sends you to the actual sites. Open them in a new tab, do the search, and write down what you find. Your notes stay in your browser.

Task 1. Find a service in the AZ Links finder.

  1. Open the AZ Links service search.
  2. Choose Maricopa County and a topic that would help an older adult who cannot get to appointments.
  3. Write the name of one agency, what it provides, and how a patient reaches it.
What a usable note looks like
A usable note has three parts: the agency name, the specific service in plain words, and the contact route with a phone number or intake page. If your note says transportation services, it is still a category. If it names the agency, the service, and how the patient gets in the door, you can put it on a plan of care.

Task 2. Find two programs at the Area Agency on Aging, Region One.

  1. Open the Region One resources page and the programs menu.
  2. Find one program for a family caregiver who is exhausted, and one for an older adult with depression or anxiety.
  3. Write both names and one sentence each on who qualifies or how referral works.
Compare with a strong response
Family Caregiver Support is the program aimed at the caregiver rather than the patient, and ElderVention is the behavioral health program for older adults. A strong note also captures the 24 hour Senior HELP LINE at 602-264-4357, because it is the one number a patient or family can call at any hour when the coordinator is not available.

Task 3. Put it together.

Pick one of the services you found and write two sentences: what problem it solves for the patient, and how you would make the referral so it does not die in a voicemail.

Compare with a strong response
A strong answer names the problem in the patient's terms, names the service, and describes a warm handoff: the call made while the patient is present, the introduction, the contact written on the plan of care, and a date to check whether the service was actually received. That last step is the one most people skip, and it is the one that tells you whether the referral worked.

If you can do this for four different needs, you have most of question 3 of the short paper.
