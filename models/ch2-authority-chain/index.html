Read down the columns. The organizations that run on tax dollars are the only ones that can compel anyone — but their reach stops at a border and they answer to elected leadership. The ones that run on donations or an endowment move freely and independently, but they can only persuade or fund, and their money is never guaranteed.

Funding → Authority → What it can (and can’t) do

That single chain is what separates every organization in this chapter. On the quiz, don’t memorize labels — ask each scenario three questions: Where does the money come from? What authority does that buy? What does that authority let this person do — and stop them from doing?
