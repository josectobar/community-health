Barriers & Bridges Sorter

Every scenario below is new — none of them appear on the quiz. Practice the reasoning move: decide whether the obstacle is about understanding, about getting there and affording it, or whether it is actually a solution that helps patients connect to care.

The three categories

  • Communication barrierSomething that blocks a patient from understanding or acting on health information — language, jargon, low health literacy, mistrust.
  • Access / structural barrierSomething outside the conversation that blocks care itself — cost, transportation, distance, insurance policy, fear tied to immigration status.
  • Communication bridgeA strategy or resource that helps close the gap — plain language, visuals, interpreters, community health workers, peer educators, mobile clinics, culturally competent care.

A useful test when you're stuck: if a perfect interpreter walked into the room right now, would the problem go away? If yes, it's a communication barrier. If the patient still can't get to the clinic or still can't pay, it's access or structural.
