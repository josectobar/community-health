The Generalized Health Planning Model

Before studying specific frameworks, understand what all systematic planning models share in common.

  1. Assess: understand the community.
  2. Diagnose: identify root causes.
  3. Plan: design the intervention.
  4. Implement: launch the program.
  5. Evaluate: measure what changed.
  6. Reassess: refine and repeat.
Why does this matter? Every health planning framework — PRECEDE-PROCEED, MAPP, and others — is built on this same cycle. What makes each framework unique is how deeply it develops each step and who it involves. Understanding the shared logic first makes it much easier to learn any specific model.
