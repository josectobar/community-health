What happens when you press start

  1. You are the patient. You pick a case and read it out loud to your partner.
  2. Your partner is the provider. They explain it to you with their screen closed.
  3. You say it back in your own words, then you both open the check together.
  4. You answer two short questions and paste them into Canvas. That is your submission.
  5. Then you swap, and your partner runs this on their own device.

While they answer, listen for these

  • Plain language. If they use a technical term, do they define it in the same breath?
  • Checking understanding. Do they ask you to say it back?
  • Inviting questions. Do they ask what questions you have, not whether you have any?
  • Supporting your choice. Do they give you what you need to decide?
