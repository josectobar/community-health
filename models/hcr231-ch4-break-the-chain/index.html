Break the Chain

An infection needs all six links to connect. Remove any one of them and transmission stops, which is why public health rarely attacks a disease head-on — it looks for the weakest link. Match each measure to the link it breaks.

The virus traveled in respiratory droplets that stayed suspended in the air for up to two hours. Unvaccinated classmates inhaled those droplets through the nose and mouth, and three developed measles within 10 to 12 days.
