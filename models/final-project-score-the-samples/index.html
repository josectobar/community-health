Score the Samples

Before you write your final project responses, practice reading them the way they will be graded. You will score five sample responses using the same rubric your instructor will use on you.

The cases below are not the cases on your final project. They are built to work the same way, so what you learn here transfers without giving you answers to memorize.

What to carry into your own writing

Look back at what separated the weak responses from the strong ones. It was almost never effort or sincerity. It was three things:

  • Every lettered part of the prompt was actually answered, not just most of them.
  • The response used details from the specific scenario instead of statements that would fit any workplace or any patient.
  • Course concepts did work in the response rather than appearing as vocabulary.

When you finish drafting your final project responses, read your own writing as a grader once. Find each lettered part. If you cannot point to it, it is not there.
