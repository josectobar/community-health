Program: A county health department receives a $60,000 grant and hires a bilingual community health worker to run a weekly mobile blood pressure screening and education program at three neighborhood barbershops. The CHW conducts screenings, distributes plain-language handouts, and refers high-risk participants to a free clinic. The team tracks the number of screenings completed each week. Within three months, they expect participants to understand their blood pressure numbers; within a year, they expect improved medication adherence among participants with hypertension; and within five years, reduced cardiovascular mortality in the target neighborhood.
