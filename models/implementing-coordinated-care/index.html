One more thing. Who should write the goal line in this plan, and why does it matter?

Compare with a strong response
The patient writes it, or the coordinator writes it in the patient's own words. A goal such as walking to the mailbox without stopping tells the team what to protect and gives the patient a reason to follow a plan that is otherwise a list of chores. A goal written by the clinic, such as maintaining fluid balance, is a clinical target and belongs elsewhere in the plan.
