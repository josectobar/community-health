The case (open this first, and reopen it any time)

4 North is a 32 bed medical surgical unit at Saguaro General Hospital. Patient outcomes on the unit are slipping, and the quality committee wants to know why.

What the unit looks like right now:

  • The five hospitalists covering 4 North also carry patients on three other units, so they round on 4 North whenever they happen to reach it.
  • Nurses work three twelve hour shifts, residents rotate every four weeks, and night float covers admissions, so the people caring for a patient on Monday are rarely the same on Thursday.
  • There are no daily interdisciplinary rounds. Pharmacy, social work, and case management each round separately.
  • Most nurse to physician communication happens by text page. Nurses say they often wait hours for a reply.
  • A staff survey found that 78 percent of physicians rated collaboration with nursing as high or very high, while 31 percent of nurses said the same about physicians.
  • Two recent safety events involved a medication a patient had reacted to before, which was documented in the nursing note and never discussed out loud.
  • The unit has a nurse manager. There is no physician counterpart.
  • In interviews, staff say they like each other and believe in teamwork. They also say there is no time and no place to talk.

You have been asked to bring recommendations to the quality committee.
