3Three Kinds of Prevention Strategies

When a scenario asks "what could have prevented this?", name one of these categories and give a specific action.

Environmental

Change the physical space

Design & equipment that reduce risk.

  • Better lighting & visibility
  • Cameras, alarms
  • Locked/limited-access areas
  • Barriers or drop safes
Administrative

Change the rules & policies

Procedures, staffing, training.

  • Zero-tolerance policies
  • Reporting & follow-up
  • Staffing (never alone at night)
  • De-escalation training
Behavioral

Change how people act

Individual choices in the moment.

  • Recognizing warning signs
  • Speaking up / reporting
  • Conflict-resolution skills
  • Following safety habits
💡 Memory hook: Environmental = the place, Administrative = the policy, Behavioral = the person.
