Managing Pediatric Asthma at Home

PATIENT DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS — PEDS/RESP

Following an acute exacerbation, adherence to the prescribed pharmacologic regimen is essential to mitigate the risk of subsequent hospitalization and to optimize long-term pulmonary function.

Administer the maintenance corticosteroid via MDI with spacer BID. The rescue bronchodilator may be administered PRN, not to exceed the frequency indicated on the pharmacy label. Monitor for tachypnea, accessory muscle utilization, and desaturation; if SpO2 falls below the threshold established by the pulmonology service, seek immediate evaluation.

  • Avoid known environmental triggers and allergens.
  • Maintain adequate ambient humidity and ventilation.
  • Ensure prophylactic immunizations remain current.
  • Adhere to the scheduled follow-up appointment.

Refer to the attached spirometry results and asthma action plan for individualized parameters. Contact the pulmonology service with questions regarding the therapeutic regimen.
