◇ The problem

The kidney waitlist

More than 90,000 people in the U.S. are waiting for a kidney transplant, and fewer than 30,000 get one in a year. Many wait years on dialysis — a demanding treatment that filters the blood several times a week.

90,000+ waiting · years-long waits
◇ The idea

What "xenotransplantation" means

It's transplanting an organ from another species into a human. Here, that's a kidney from a pig whose genes have been edited to lower the chance the human body rejects it.

Cross-species · gene-edited pig organ
◇ The status

Where it stands today

It is still experimental. The first FDA-authorized clinical trials began in 2025 with a very small number of patients. It is not a proven, everyday option you can schedule. Real risks remain: the body can still reject the organ, and there is concern about disease passing from animal to human.

Experimental · rejection & cross-species risk
◇ Your job

Why this is a communication challenge

Your task is to listen, answer in plain language, and check that the patient understands. You don't need to be a medical expert. You help the patient feel part of the decision and make their own informed choice.

Listen · plain language · informed consent

One more thing — say it in your own words

Asking someone to teach it back is the hardest sentence in the whole conversation. Write the exact words you would use with Daniel. There is no grading here.

Falls flat
Does that all make sense? Any questions? — he will say yes and no, in that order, and you learn nothing.
Lands
I want to make sure I explained this well — that's on me, not you. If your daughter called tonight and asked what we talked about, what would you tell her?
