Build the team, write the objective

Planners do two things constantly: decide who belongs on the team, and state what the program is committing to. Practice both here on Copper Ridge, a former mining town of 3,800 in Gila County where diabetes-related hospitalizations have climbed for three years.

Step 1

What to take away

  • A role is defined by what someone is responsible for, not by where they work. Two people can sit in the same clinic and answer for very different things.
  • An objective without a number and a date is a goal wearing an objective's clothes.
  • Process counts what you did. Impact measures what changed in people or their surroundings. Outcome measures health itself, and takes the longest to see.
