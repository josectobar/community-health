Millbrook Team Builder

Millbrook is starting a naloxone program. Six jobs need doing. Decide who should take each one, then explain what makes that person the right fit. There is no answer key here — the reasoning is the work.

The case

Millbrook is a rural town of 18,000. Opioid overdose deaths rose from 3 to 14 in three years, and 40% of overdose patients in the emergency room have been treated more than once. Unemployment is at 14%. Overdose survivors avoid the clinic because they fear being judged. Three students were found with prescription pills on campus. Families do not know how to help a relative who is using. No naloxone is available to the public.
