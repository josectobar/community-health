The plain language clinic

Appraising a material is not about knowing big words. It is about telling when a material fails the person holding it — and then fixing it. Practice on a notice written for Copper Ridge, a former mining town of 3,800 in Gila County where a third of residents are over 65 and the nearest specialist is 40 miles away.

Step 1

Find what fails the reader

This notice was mailed to Copper Ridge households. Click any phrase you think would make it hard for a packing shed worker to use. Not every underlined phrase is a problem — some are exactly what the reader needs. Choose carefully, then check.

Notice: Low Blood Sugar

Patients receiving may experience . Individuals experiencing should and regarding regimen adjustment. Education sessions are held at

    Step 2

    Rewrite it in three sentences

    This is the shape the quiz asks for. Answer three questions for the reader, one sentence each. Use words you would say out loud to a family member who has no health training.

    What is the danger?

    What should I do? Include a number or a time if you can.

    When should I get help?

    Plain language check

    • All three sentences are written
    • Sentences stay under about 15 words
    • No leftover technical words
    • Sentence 2 tells the reader a specific amount or time
    • Sentence 3 names something the reader could notice in their own body

    One way to write it

    Diabetes medicine can make your blood sugar drop too low. If you feel shaky, sweaty, or confused, eat or drink something sugary right away — half a cup of juice or a few glucose tablets. If you do not feel better in 15 minutes, or you cannot swallow or think clearly, call 911.

    Notice what changed. The reader is spoken to directly. The symptom words are things a person can feel. “Rapid-acting carbohydrate” became two examples you could find in a kitchen. And the specialist referral was cut entirely, because it was not usable here, replaced by a next step that is. Your version does not need to match this one. It needs to pass the same five checks.
